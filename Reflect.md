This is the definition of a Daily Reflection is:

1. Target a problem before in your life
2. Give a “simple solution” you can start tomorrow
3. This “simple solution” **can be achieved tomorrow** and behavior will last for a while in the future
4. A good “simple solution” may need time to think, but worth it
5. The “simple solution” here means you can put a task into the Notion, and start to do it from the next day on, when there are too many tasks, even removing tasks is necessary after the “reflection”

The difference between reflection and thinking:

Reflection: Reflect on the status quo (to understand, destroy, reconstruct, increase the sensibility toward the backward, toward things that already exit. Reflection is very important, it's like the way to protect and keep your own culture and tradition):

Thinking: Think about something new (questions you don’t familiar with, have to learn something new, or think about some new ideas):
